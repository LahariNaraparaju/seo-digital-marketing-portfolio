 🤖 AI Prompt Engineering Frameworks & Workflows

This section demonstrates how I leverage generative AI tools (like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini) not as automatic text generators, but as highly sophisticated assistants to optimize my digital marketing and writing workflows.
 🛠️ How I Use AI in My Daily Workflow
Instead of relying on simple, single-line prompts that output generic and robotic text, I design multi-layered context-rich prompt frameworks. This allows me to scale operations while strictly keeping a human-first approach:
- Advanced Content Auditing:Building custom prompts that analyze raw content drafts against Google's E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) guidelines to spot quality gaps and improve search compliance.
- Competitor & Content Outlining: Creating structured prompt frameworks that analyze high-ranking competitor pages, extract semantic keyword patterns, and generate comprehensive, high-level layouts for new articles.
- Tone & Style Polishing (AI-as-an-Editor):Designing role-based prompts that instruct AI to act strictly as an *Assistant Editor*. This framework focuses on fixing grammar, eliminating passive voice, and enhancing readability while carefully preserving my unique human voice, personal stories, and emotional hooks.
